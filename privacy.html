Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Dutoza Status Saver ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App works completely offline.

2. Permissions

  • Storage / Media access: Only to read WhatsApp Status files that you grant access to via the system folder picker, and to save files you choose.
  • Notifications: Optional, only to notify you when a status is saved.

3. Data Storage

All saved statuses are stored only on your device. We have no access to them.

4. Third Parties

The App does not use any analytics, advertising SDKs that collect personal data, or third-party tracking.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at: support@dutoza.com
