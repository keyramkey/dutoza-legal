Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Dutoza Status Saver ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

Summary: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App works completely offline.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, usage data, or location data. The App functions entirely on your device without sending data to any server.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Storage / Media access — Only to read WhatsApp Status files that you explicitly grant access to using the system folder picker, and to save files you choose to download.
  • Notifications — Optional. Used solely to notify you when a status has been saved successfully.

We never access any other files or folders on your device beyond what you authorize.

3. Data Storage

All statuses you save are stored exclusively on your device (in the Pictures or Movies folder under "Dutoza Status"). We have no access to these files and they never leave your phone.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
